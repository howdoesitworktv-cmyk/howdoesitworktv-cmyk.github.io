Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Pix Studio is a personal tool used only by the creator of Pix Knows Why to publish videos to his own accounts. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

What we access

  • TikTok: basic profile (display name, username, avatar) to show which account a video will post to; permission to publish videos you choose to post; and a list of your videos with their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos and thumbnails, and read-only access to your channel's video statistics and analytics.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels and read their insights.

How it is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server of ours; Pix Studio has no servers. Data goes only to the platform it came from, through that platform's official API.

What we don't do

We don't sell, share or rent any data, don't use it for advertising, and don't collect data about anyone other than the connected account owner. Pix Studio has no cookies, tracking or analytics of its own.

Keeping and deleting data

Statistics are kept as long as the videos exist, to compare how videos perform. To delete everything, revoke the app's access in the platform's settings; local data can be deleted at any time, or on request by email.

Children

Pix Studio is not directed at children and is used by one adult account owner.
